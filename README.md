# Noor-e-Quran AI – Taleem-e-Noor AI

**Noor-e-Quran AI** is an Artificial Intelligence-based Islamic education system designed to connect users' everyday questions with relevant verses of the Holy Quran. The system aims to make Quranic guidance more accessible through AI-powered semantic search, multilingual support, voice search, and an interactive question-answering feature.

The project helps users explore Quranic verses related to their questions in simple, natural language, making Quranic learning easier and more accessible for students, young people, and the general public.

## Key Features

- **Semantic Search:** Finds Quranic verses based on the meaning and context of a user's question.
- **Tri-Lingual Support:** Enables interaction in three supported languages.
- **Voice Search:** Allows users to ask questions using voice input.
- **Question-Answering System:** Helps users discover relevant Quranic references for their questions.
- **User-Friendly Interface:** Provides a simple and accessible way to explore Quranic content.

## Project Objectives

- Promote accessible Quranic learning through modern AI technology.
- Help users discover relevant Quranic verses for everyday concerns.
- Reduce language barriers in searching for Quranic guidance.
- Encourage users to read and reflect on Quranic verses with their references and context.

## Technology and Approach

The project focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing (NLP), semantic search, multilingual interaction, and speech recognition where implemented.

## Purpose

The main purpose of Noor-e-Quran AI is to combine modern technology with Islamic education, helping users explore the Holy Quran in a convenient and meaningful way.

**Note:** This system is intended for educational and Quranic exploration purposes. Users should consult qualified Islamic scholars for complex interpretations and religious rulings.
